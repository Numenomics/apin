# Dual-Horizon 5-Lane GLM-5.3-Flash Campaign Record (2026-11-21)

Five parallel autonomous campaigns (`GLM-5.3-Flash`, `--dims 6,20 --repeats 2`, dual-horizon `H_short=150 / H_long=1000`, serialized via `harness/queue.py`) across all algorithmic islands **A0–A4**, followed by a contention-free real-Stan comparison on 5 public Stan posteriors (`blr`, `eight_schools_noncentered`, `eight_schools_centered`, `gp_regr`, `garch11`, 3 reps × 4 chains × 5000 draws each) scored on both **Track L1** (cost units) and **Track L2** (wall-clock `ESS/s`).

> **Claim-wording note:** Passing a model means *"passed the 0.1-SD bench gate at this budget"*, never *"is correct"*, *"exact"*, *"unbiased"*, or *"proven"*.

### Per-Model Highlights
- **`blr`:** `a1_glm53_dual` reached **`0.0134 / cost`** (**4.7× `a1_diag`**, **3.5× CmdStan NUTS**), showing the huge payoff of spectrally damped dense MAD-whitened covariance on correlated posteriors.
- **`gp_regr`:** `a3_glm53_symp` reached **`0.0650 / cost`** (**1.62× CmdStan**, #1 single-model efficiency), followed by `a3_glm53_pdmp_1h` (`0.0583/cost`, 1.45× CmdStan) and `a4_glm53_dual` (`0.0535/cost`, 1.33× CmdStan).
- **`garch11`:** `a1_glm53_dual` reached **`0.0190 / cost`** (**1.88× CmdStan**, #1), followed by `a3_glm53_pdmp_1h` (`0.0172/cost`, 1.70×) and `a4_glm53_dual` (`0.0170/cost`, 1.68×).
- **`eight_schools_centered`:** `a2_glm53_dual` achieved the tightest R-hat of all 8 arms (**`1.014`** on `lp__`, vs `1.017` `a1_diag`, `1.270` PDMP, and `ESS 120` CmdStan).
- **Lane A4 diagnostic:** `a4_glm53_dual` passed 3/5 real Stan models (`eight_schools_noncentered`, `gp_regr`, `garch11`), while our automatic finite-difference Jacobian checker in `harness/judge.py` flagged `blr` (`log_det=-86.22` vs FD `-86.32`) and `eight_schools_centered` rep2 (`log_det=-137.02` vs FD `-134.49`) — pointing to extreme scale/conditioning drift in the triangular/SAS map under wide Stan parameter scales.
- **Short-horizon warmup trade-off (`A1` & `A3`):** Cutting warmup too aggressively for `H_short=150` boosted `blr`/`garch11` massively (`a1_glm53_dual`: `1.97×` overall) but left `eight_schools_noncentered` slightly unmixed (`R-hat 1.031`), and 1-step GHMC (`a3_glm53_symp`, 5.5× synthetic) under-mixed `blr` (`R-hat 1.514`) and barely missed `garch11` (`bound 0.101`).
